# Held-out set 4 — author notes

All names, people and conversations are invented. Line numbers are 0-based.

**Encoding choices that apply to the whole set.** I tagged `end_and_new_patient` on the negative scripts too (h4-01, 04, 06, 08, 09, 10, 21), because they test that axis even though no `next_patient` role appears. Where a speaker fixes something, I listed only the fixed version as a fact, on the fix line, and did not list the wrong version (the note is expected to keep both lines). Any doubt words in a line where I listed no fact are left unmarked.

**h4-01** (initial, low back, 17 lines). Tests clinician lines with no cue phrase: an examination finding (line 8), advice (13) and safety-netting (14). Line 12 is the clinician hedging ("I suspect … probably") and is not marked. The patient voices doubt in the middle of a long line (6, "whether it's every night I really couldn't say"). The consultation ends with a goodbye and no one new comes in (negative). Next patient: none. Fixes: none. Patient doubt: line 6.
