The child workflows keep their own contracts — v32.3's exactly TWO gate-relevant, precisely-scoped child edits are `/peer-loop`'s orchestrated-CODE early tail accept-close branch and `/fix`'s advisory fix-delta self-check line (`/review-loop` byte-untouched by v32.3 — v47.0 adds one pointer sentence at its cap bullet, no gate change; `/review`'s own v32.3 edits, the fan-out predicate + the exhaustive-siblings rule, change how findings are produced, never a gate seam; the early-close branch is inert standalone — no keys resolve outside the orchestrated composer seat — and the self-check binds every `/fix` leg but is advisory-only and gate-neutral); standalone runs of those loops keep strict/ask behavior. Absent or malformed keys FAIL CLOSED to `strict`/`ask`/`off` (v33.0 companions: `peer-max=` absent → the +3 budget alone; `notify=` absent → `all`; `profile-fallback=` absent → the usage-exhaustion pause; under `router=on` a ROUTED role's exhaustion is re-picked first — three per run on one counter for every role — with `profile-fallback=` the Claude roles' last resort, never the codex peer's: SKILL → `## Usage-exhaustion recovery (claude pools)`, the router branch); the full seam-by-seam contract is canonical (`## Safety gates — the loop PAUSES, it never bypasses` + `## Termination`). The restore pair `gates=strict; cap-raise=ask` reproduces the surface-everything ceiling per run (the pre-v31 shape; `high-auto` is INERT under `gates=strict`; an absent key fails closed to `off`); `gates=fix-biased; cap-raise=follow` reproduces the v32.5 shape. Composer-seat dispositions are NEVER delegated to a spawned role (delegation moves work, never the seat — the v47.0 verify-then-fix leg, §6, moves only the fix's TIMING for qualifying MED and LOW `Fix-now` findings: the records stay yours); the in-executor `/review-loop`→`/fix` chain is a different, unchanged seam — never conflate the two. Blind window: a running executor keeps its spawn-time policy carry until its activation ends — a mid-phase `gates=`/`cap-raise=`/`high-auto=` change that must bind the CURRENT executor requires hard-pause + respawn (a `high-auto` DISABLE binds the composer seat the moment its `config-revoke` lands).

**Executor-verified triage is the routing authority (operator-set):** a peer's severity/recommendation labels are INPUTS, never routing truth — peer labels are demonstrably noisy (docs-wording findings arriving MED/HIGH; severity jitter across rounds, both run-evidenced). The verifying executor (resumed per §6; premium build context) re-classifies each finding WITH EVIDENCE: materiality = `docs-only | behavioral | regression | invalid`, plus a verified severity — every downgrade/upgrade carries a one-line evidence note journaled on the round, and the peer's original labels stay recorded beside the verified ones (so `/retro` can measure label inflation). Disposition reads the round's VERIFIED-TUPLE RECORD wherever it lives — the `#### LEG 1 verified tuples` block OR a finding already carrying the executor-authored tuple on its record-and-end gate record (canonical step 4's skip-leg-1 shortcut) — NEVER the peer's own block (same-round peer and leg-1 blocks coexist; the round-31 mis-route disposed from peer labels and auto-routed an Accept). On a round whose block carries `Leg route: verify-then-fix` (§6) the fix is already applied when you read it: check every tuple against the leg's conditions, then write the SAME per-finding disposition pairs — the ONE place a disposition record follows its act; that line beside ANY tuple that did not qualify (a HIGH or CRIT, a `rec=` other than `Fix-now`, a regression, a scope expansion, production impact, declared uncertainty, a round at its cap) is a review-integrity pause, never a disposition. `Leg route: verify-only`, or no such line, is the two-leg route: dispose first, then leg 2. Gate routing then keys on the VERIFIED classification, under the `gates=` mode (the key that owns finding disposition; `cap-raise=` alone owns actual AT-CAP stops — the pre-cap early tail accept-close below is the one gates-sensitive exception): `docs-only`/`invalid` → auto-resolve under `fix-biased`/`executor`, logged AND notified (§7 notification — zero silent auto-actions; under `strict` they SURFACE with the verified classification — strict auto-resolves nothing); `behavioral` → the fix-biased rules below, or under `executor` the executor's `rec=` outside the canonical residual list; any `regression` → never silently Accepted or Deferred (auto-fix under fix-biased/executor when in-scope/non-production, otherwise pause); a VERIFIED CRIT always pauses; scope-expansion routing is unaffected by materiality — scope is the operator's dimension. v33.0 D9 on the tuple: the `surface=production|test-harness|docs` qualifier (this repo's own validators are NEVER `test-harness`); after the 2nd consecutive same-family round whose survivors are all `test-harness` at MED-and-below the executor's `rec=` defaults to `Accept-with-record` (followed under `executor`; a HIGH harness finding keeps its normal route); a finding whose sole target is the currency of records the fix leg just refreshed is `invalid` at LEG 1 — auto-resolved, no fix leg.

**Cap raises (`cap-raise=ask|auto-plus-one|follow|executor`; DEFAULT `executor`, operator-set — v33.0 D2; absent/malformed → `ask`):** a cap has exactly THREE legal outcomes — RAISE, SURFACE-AND-ASK, or the RECORDED `cap-accept` closure (under `cap-raise=follow` or `executor` ONLY; `ask`/`auto-plus-one` keep the pre-v32 two) — never a silent close: a flat/rising cap-stop with no co-incident gate still SURFACES, the ask PAUSES INDEPENDENTLY (the run holds at the cap while it is open), and accept-closure is legal ONLY as the explicit recorded transition — an unrecorded close remains the violation (r9 PR-MED-019). Under `auto-plus-one` (retained), auto-raise the cap by EXACTLY +1 — ONCE per pass, act-and-inform (journal `OWNERSHIP: auto-disposition cap-raise=+1` + its paired `NOTIFY:` per the DISPOSITION-TIME checklist below, `mode=immediate`) — ONLY when ALL of: every verified finding from the cap round is already applied (nothing pending), the trajectory is CONVERGING (severity-weighted finding count strictly declining round-over-round), and the reviewer/executor recommendation is continue; a second raise on the same pass, a flat/rising trajectory, or pending unapplied findings MUST-PAUSE as under `ask`. Under **`follow`** (the v32.5 DEFAULT — D2): FOLLOW the residue-routing recommendation already computed at every surfaced cap, act-and-inform — raise-recommended ⇒ the `cap-raise=+1` pair above; accept-recommended ⇒ `cap-accept` — journal `OWNERSHIP: auto-disposition cap-accept=close` + paired `NOTIFY: … class=cap-accept mode=immediate`. Precedence is the canonical PINNED TOTAL ORDER, fail-closed, less-autonomous-wins: (1) pending unapplied findings ⇒ SURFACE; (2) stalemate ⇒ STOP; (3) CRIT/HIGH regression ⇒ SURFACE; (4) tail-signature ⇒ SURFACE (2 consecutive docs-only / same-family-defensive-completion rounds, no CRIT/HIGH regression — existing skew vocabulary); (5) accept-close; (6) raise — records-only residue that ALSO matches the tail signature SURFACES, never accept-closes. Hard budget: max +2 cumulative follow-raises per pass, then SURFACE; a missing/ambiguous/conflicting recommendation ⇒ SURFACE. Genuine non-convergence belongs to the operator (canonical: `## Termination`). Under **`executor`** (DEFAULT — D2): the recommendation input is the executor's **`Cap verdict:`** line from the LEG-1 brief (§6 — `raise <+n> | accept | defer-residue` + materiality `production-behavioral | test-harness | docs-only` + one-line evidence; missing/ambiguous/conflicting ⇒ SURFACE, fail-closed) and `follow`'s pinned total order runs with exactly TWO differences: the verdict replaces composer-computed residue routing, and item (4) — the tail signature — is DECIDED BY THE VERDICT (accept ⇒ `cap-accept=close`; raise ⇒ raise) instead of surfacing; items (1) pending ⇒ SURFACE, (2) stalemate ⇒ STOP, (3) CRIT/HIGH regression ⇒ SURFACE, (5)/(6) unchanged. A `raise` verdict is exactly ONE `cap-raise=+1` pair per cap event — `raise +n` with n > 1 is honoured one increment per cap event, the next event re-consulting the (possibly refreshed) verdict — within the executor-mode per-pass budget of **+3 cumulative**, after which `peer-max` (`Loop config:`, default 12; absent → the +3 budget alone) SURFACES ONCE per pass with the verdict attached (the operator may lift it for this pass); `defer-residue` ⇒ `… cap-accept=close pass=<p> residue=deferred` + the residue as a Deferred item with `Risk if deferred:`. Under `executor` the PRE-CAP early tail accept-close branch below is NOT EVALUATED — it neither closes nor surfaces (its predicate is `fix-biased`+`follow` only); a mid-pass tail signature continues to the next round and the verdict decides at cap. The intro's "flat/rising cap-stop … still SURFACES" is the no-silent-close rule (the cap-stop reaches your seat as an EVENT); under `executor` the verdict disposes it — the trajectory is neither a veto nor an ask. **Per-pass keys (D3 — MECHANIZED on the record, your duty to supply):** the peer cap is a per-PASS budget — the counter restarts at 1 at every `/peer-loop` start (phase or gate/slice); at each start you MINT an invocation-unique `pass=<phase-or-slice>.p<N>` id (N = that slice's invocation ordinal — a re-invocation over the same slice never collides) and every peer activation's `ROLE: start role=peer` carries `pass=<id> peer_round=<k> cap=<N>` — wrapper spawns via `LOOP_PASS`/`LOOP_PEER_ROUND`/`LOOP_CAP` (§8), composer-seat codex rounds through the launcher via the SAME three variables (§4 step 8 — the launcher puts them on the start it writes), hand-route codex peers via `emit ROLE:start … --log <probe-log> pass= peer_round= cap=` — ALL THREE or NONE (a standalone `/peer-loop` emits none; a partial set refuses); the helper sequences them against the ACTUAL prior log (`peer_round ≤ cap`, consecutive rounds — the inconclusive-retry same-round exception after a `ROLE: end result=` naming `inconclusive`/`incomplete` — and `cap=` equal to the pass's seed plus its recorded same-pass transitions; a pass with no prior keyed start seeds from any `peer_round ≤ cap` — the v32.5-started-run grandfather). Every cap transition you emit (`cap-raise=+1`, `cap-accept=close`, `gate-disposition key=cap-raise|peer-cap|peer-cap-accept`) carries the two-key bundle `pass=<p> cap=<N>` (both or neither; never `peer_round=` — NEITHER on the in-session `/review-loop`'s cap verdict, which from v47.0 you RECORD in the unkeyed form with the loop and its round in `detail=`: SKILL → `## Termination`, the cap row), and on a KEYED transition `--log <probe-log>` is LOAD-BEARING — the helper validates the recorded `cap=` against the pass's budget (an automatic raise records budget+1, an operator raise its larger cap, an accept-close the current budget) and a lie never moves the budget. At `round == cap` the branch is SKIPPED — the at-cap contract above owns the outcome per mode: under this pass's validated `follow`, tail at cap SURFACES (item (4)); `ask` surfaces-and-asks; `auto-plus-one` keeps its unchanged three-condition raise predicate, independent of `gates=`. Executor-owned `/review-loop` chains, plan-review passes, opt-in executor-seat passes, and standalone `/peer-loop` are all ineligible (canonical: `## Termination` + `/peer-loop` step 4).

**DISPOSITION-TIME — at EVERY composer-seat disposition, EVERY severity, EVERY route; the pair is owed BY THE DISPOSITION — it fires even when no verify leg ran:**

1. ONE invocation emits the OWNERSHIP+NOTIFY pair. Auto route: `python3 scripts/loop-journal.py emit OWNERSHIP:auto-disposition --run <runkey> --role composer --log <probe-log> --epoch "<activation id>" --round <n> --id <finding-ID> severity=<route class> [verified=<class>] [authority_check=<config_hash> authority_epoch=<n>] gates=<the run's resolved mode — executor|fix-biased> detail="<rationale>"`.
