- Cliniko workflow safeguards: **sleep and screen-lock pause** (D5 as amended by the practitioner, 2026-09-28, after the morning smoke's step 5). Built; the composer's test run and review are still to come.
  - **Why:** on the practitioner's Modern Standby machine (S0 low power idle; S3 unavailable), Start → Power → Sleep did NOT pause a desktop recording. Windows never sent the window the classic `WM_POWERBROADCAST` / `PBT_APMSUSPEND` that Phase 5 relied on, and the Phase 5 test had posted that message itself.
  - **What (new `system_events.py`; `ui/main_window.py`, `app.py`):**
    - `app.main` registers the main window with `RegisterSuspendResumeNotification(hwnd, DEVICE_NOTIFY_WINDOW_HANDLE)`, so suspend is delivered on Modern Standby. The classic broadcast is still handled, and a second suspend finds the recording already paused.
    - It also registers with `WTSRegisterSessionNotification(hwnd, NOTIFY_FOR_THIS_SESSION)`. A `WTS_SESSION_LOCK` (Win+L, a lid close, standby with sign-in required) pauses ANY recording, linked or not, with the new reason `locked` and the cue "Paused - the computer was locked." A linked recording also gets the block.
    - An unlock resumes nothing, and a suspend or lock ends a clicked "Resume previous" still waiting for its note's report.
    - Both registrations are given back on close, at quit and after a failed start. A refusal never raises, is logged (`suspend_notification` / `lock_notification`), and is shown on the status line and the Session screen.
    - The side panel and the page's block show "The computer was locked." No protocol change: block reasons are pattern-checked codes.
    - **Locked until unlock** (codex round 51 PR-MED-300): a Resume or "Resume previous" click still on its way through Chrome when the lock arrived could otherwise restart the recording behind the locked screen.
      - The lock message now sets a flag during its own dispatch. Every Resume path (the Session tab, the hotkey, Chrome's `resume`, a waiting "Resume previous") — and, since the hardening review (rounds 53–54, MED-039 and MED-052), every Start, the side panel's and the Session tab's — is refused with `locked` — "The computer is locked - sign in, then press it again." — until the unlock clears it. The unlock resumes nothing.
      - A missed unlock is re-checked with Windows (`WTSSessionInfoEx`) once the flag is 5 s old. If Windows cannot say, the refusal is `lock_unknown` and names the escape.
      - There is no suspend flag (residue in the threat model). Tests: the peer's ordering (lock, then the delayed click, then the note's report), every desktop path, unlock, a missed unlock, an unanswered check, a refused registration, and the child process's flag after the lock and after the unlock.
