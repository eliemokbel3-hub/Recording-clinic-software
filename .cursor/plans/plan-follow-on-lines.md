## Planned Workflow Summary

### Flow 1 — Scratch and premises (the agent)
- A fresh scratch library re-implements the router with a reason code per line, and proves it equals the shipped provider before any candidate is switched on. The three candidates are then read at text level (each script's own words).

### Flow 2 — Audio (the agent, on the practitioner's "start now")
- The seven sets are rebuilt outside the repository and transcribed once, the two key sets twice. The shipped product runs over them in the gate configuration; its result is compared fact by fact with 0.3.7's saved outputs, and the difference between two takes of one set gives the noise floor.

### Flow 3 — The map (the agent)
- Every failure on the seven sets gets one cause from the closed list. The candidates are read at audio level with suggestions off, each beside its harm columns.

### Flow 4 — Real speech (the practitioner)
- A reviewed, hash-frozen, numbers-only script runs over the kept recordings from a normal terminal with both builds closed, and prints where the 0.3.7 rules act and where the candidates would. The practitioner pastes the numbers.

### Flow 5 — Records and the decision (both)
- The map, the candidate tables and the 0.3.7 harm go into the harness document and the findings register. The practitioner chooses: build a form (an amendment to this plan), hand the map on, or close with no change. The plan closes on every exit.

- **D2 — Scratch only, fresh, with two premises.** `L/ffl_lib.py` copies the replay, capture, alignment and counter helpers from `L/afq_lib.py` (which stays untouched as 0.3.7's method record) and re-implements the shipped loop — hop included — in a provider subclass injected through `HarnessInputs.note_provider_factory`, emitting one reason code per utterance (cued, joined, carried, followed, refused-by-guard, dropped, each with the test that decided it). "Equals" means, per encounter: the whole note (every assertion, its coordinates, section key and order), the check warnings, the `EncounterMetrics`, the reference reading and the credit sets (the comparison `afq-measure.py:600-612` made). Premise 1 (text level): with every candidate off it equals the shipped provider on all seven sets, 0 differences. Premise 2 (replay): over every saved transcript it equals the shipped provider, 0 differences — text level never exercised switch S, a carry after a join, blank segments or label disagreement (`AFQ:1322`). A difference is a finding against the scratch. Premise 1 comes before any text-level number and Premise 2 before any audio-level number the scratch computes (the traces, the map, the candidates). Task 2.2's baseline is the shipped product's own run with no scratch provider, so it does not wait for Premise 2. Premise 2 runs straight after transcription (Task 2.1) and again over a retry take before that take is used; Task 1.2's tables are provisional until Premise 2 passes, and are regenerated after it.
  Alternatives considered: reusing `afq_lib.HopProvider` (it predates the product's hop and is a method record); a harness flag (changes shipped code before a decision).
- **D3 — Where things live.** Built sets `C:\ffl-sets\<set>`; pickled transcripts `C:\ffl-sets\docs\<set>` (take 1) and `C:\ffl-sets\docs-t2\<set>` (take 2, `heldout-2` and `heldout-6` only) and `C:\ffl-sets\docs-t3\<set>` (a retry take, only where D4 asks for one; `afq-transcribe.py:212` skips an existing pickle, so every take has its own folder and none is overwritten or deleted); logs, traces and tables under `L/ffl-out/`. All synthetic. The composer runs the builds and transcriptions and copies into `L/` whatever a scoped leg must read. The practitioner deletes `C:\ffl-sets\` at the close.
- **D4 — Reproduce first, then a noise floor.** `L/afq-transcribe.py` is reused unchanged. The shipped product (no scratch provider) runs in the gate configuration (`--suggestions confirm-all --prose`, rule v6, the validation config) over take 1 of all seven sets and take 2 of the two. The result is saved per fact as index, material, verdict, warned, the adjusted silent flag (`silent_omission_v2`), the raw silent flag and the residue flags, once with the reply credit and once without. Per fact, take 1 is compared with `L/afq-out/built/<set>/product.json` on its five fields, `QT` against the default credit and `shipped` against the calculation without the reply credit. Fixed before any run: take 1 is ALWAYS this plan's baseline of record, whatever it shows; no take is chosen by its result. Where a set's take 1 differs from the archive, one retry take is made (`docs-t3`) and used only to mark each differing fact "also on the retry" or "not on the retry" (the 2026-11-10 lesson); the differences are recorded by name, fact by fact. The noise floors are measured on this 0.3.7 baseline, per set and per measure, between two takes of the set: takes 1 and 2 for `heldout-2` and `heldout-6`, and take 1 and the retry for any other set that has one. The fact-level floor is the number of material facts whose reported verdict, or whose RAW silent flag under the default credit (with the reply credit), differs between the two takes; it is the floor for the raw-silent column, and the same count taken without the reply credit is the floor for that column.
