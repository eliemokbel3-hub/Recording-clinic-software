## Goal
The practitioner compared this scribe with Heidi Health and finds Heidi hears more and writes more clinically. Heidi runs cloud models; this app's founding rule is no cloud processing of audio or transcripts, so the only levers are local. Two facts frame what is possible: the recogniser is the lever for "hears more", and the synthetic TTS sets (pooled WER 0.0121 under `medium`) cannot show a real-speech gap — only the consented kept recordings can, numbers only, practitioner-run. "Writes more clinically" is mostly this app's design (a template-mapped, transcript-grounded note that never invents a clinical sentence) and is the NEXT plan (PLAN.md Phase 3B), not this one.

This plan: (1) makes GPU decoding work on this laptop's RTX 4050 (6 GB) — the NVIDIA libraries declared, bundled and preloaded by absolute path, the provider strict about model AND device, a CPU fallback that is never silent; (2) measures `large-v3@cuda` and `medium@cuda` against `medium@cpu` under a selection rule fixed before any download — the five synthetic sets as a floor, a blind console adjudication of real-speech disagreements as the decision; (3) on outcome (i) ships `large-v3@cuda` with `medium@cpu` beside it, version 0.3.6 and validation run 9 on a fresh blind `heldout-6` (run 8 is `plan-validation-run8.md`'s and comes first — order A); on (ii) ships the GPU as a speed setting for `medium`; on (iii) records No change and ships nothing.

### Codebase Integration Notes
Established on 2026-11-06 from the agent shell (read-only; the probe used the benchmark's own non-clinical SAPI sample, never clinical audio):

The library exposes a device count and the compute types ONLY — no card name, no memory statistics (`ctranslate2/__init__.py:24-45`).
- **What the wheel ships and what the bundle carries.** `ctranslate2.dll` (59 MB), `cudnn64_9.dll` (266 KB — a SHIM, FileVersion **9.10.2.21**) and `libiomp5md.dll`; the installed bundle carries exactly these three (`C:\Program Files\ClinikoScribe\_internal\ctranslate2`). `ctranslate2/__init__.py` `ctypes.CDLL`s EVERY `*.dll` in its own folder on import (so the shim is loaded at every app start, whatever the setting) and `add_dll_directory`s that folder. The import table of `ctranslate2.dll` has no static import of nvcuda, cublas or cudnn: all are loaded at run time by name — `nvcuda.dll` (the driver, in System32), `cublas64_12.dll` (lazily, at first use), and the shim loads `cudnn_adv64_9`, `cudnn_cnn64_9`, `cudnn_graph64_9`, `cudnn_ops64_9` (which load `cudnn_engines_precompiled64_9`, `cudnn_engines_runtime_compiled64_9`, `cudnn_heuristic64_9`).

### D1 — Candidates and the selection rule (decided 2026-11-06; fixed in this plan's commit before any download — C4)
Candidates: **`medium@cuda`** (`int8_float16`) and **`large-v3@cuda`** (Systran, `WHISPER_CANDIDATES`' pinned revision; re-fetched by the practitioner). The comparison is **`medium@cpu`** (run 7's configuration). Not re-measured: `large-v3-turbo`, `distil-large-v3` (Excluded).

The rule, mechanical, applied once at Task D:
0. *Completeness premise* — larger-model D1 rule 0: every expected encounter `measured`, no `[error]` line, read from the report itself.
1. *Eligibility on this computer:* (a) app-shape RTF < 1.0 on the GPU, median of three idle runs (`benchmark --app-shape --device cuda`, source-only); (b) **VRAM ≤ 4096 MiB** — measured IN-PROCESS by Task 0.1's probe, which shells out to `nvidia-smi --query-gpu=memory.used` before the load and again after the warm decode while the model is still resident, and prints the difference (the benchmark's workers are subprocesses that have exited before any outside reading — `benchmark.py:723-743`, `:779-786` — so a reading around the benchmark invocation measures nothing; peer round 1 PR-MED-001; the library has no memory statistics; the card's total includes the desktop; the bar is the practitioner's choice by analogy with the RAM bar, on a 6 GB card shared with the desktop) — **aggregation (fixed here, C4; peer round 3 PR-MED-012): the VRAM bar is read against the MAXIMUM of three valid probe differences (a ceiling is a ceiling), load against the MEDIAN of the three benchmark load times (as the RTF), each ≤ its bar; a reading is INVALID only when the probe or the worker exited non-zero or printed no number — it is repeated, with a BOUND of six attempts per configuration per instrument (three readings plus three retries; `_run_worker` raises on a failed worker, `benchmark.py:741-743`); a configuration that cannot produce three valid readings within the bound is INELIGIBLE on rule 1, recorded with its failure codes and the valid readings it did produce, its dependent measurements (its A.5 sets, its B.2 adjudication) are skipped, and Task D evaluates the remaining configurations (peer round 4 PR-MED-013); no reading is ever discarded for its value** — and load ≤ 30 s; (c) the mel assertion passes and word timestamps return words; (d) silent material omissions over the five seen sets ≤ `medium@cpu`'s (26).
2. *Quality floor (synthetic):* the 50 PASS rule v6 (= rule v5's predicates and ceilings; order A — before Task 1.4a creates `rule-v6.json`, A.5 runs the candidates under rule v5, whose 50's row is the same), and material facts `wrong` summed over the five seen sets ≤ `medium@cpu`'s + 1 (≤ 8, with `medium@cpu`'s count re-read from run 8 at Task 0.2) — the practitioner's dated loosening of the predecessor's "must beat `medium`" (2026-11-06), because the TTS set is at its resolution limit and real speech now decides. 3. *Quality decision (real speech, D5):* `large-v3@cuda` is chosen when, over the adjudicated disagreements against `medium@cpu`, the count judged "large-v3 right" is **at least 60% of (large-v3 right + medium right)** — `n` (neither / unsure) and `s` (same meaning) are excluded from the denominator — over at least **30** such judgements from at least **5 CONTRIBUTING consultations** (a consultation contributes when it holds at least one decisive judgement — `1` or `2`; one consultation's thirty votes plus four consultations of `n` / `s` do not qualify) on a **COMPLETE** run (one the practitioner did not end with `q`; an incomplete run is ineligible and is re-run in full — peer round 1 PR-MED-005). A judgement threshold the practitioner confirmed on 2026-11-06, not a significance test (18 of 30 against an even split has a one-sided binomial p of about 0.18 — stated so nobody reads it as proof).
4. *Adopting the GPU without a model change* (`medium@cuda`) needs: the five synthetic reports equal to `medium@cpu`'s on every verdict line (rule v5 prints four — all four; words may differ; verdicts, failing ids and residue members, R1's marked / silent split included, may not). 5. Nothing else is read.
