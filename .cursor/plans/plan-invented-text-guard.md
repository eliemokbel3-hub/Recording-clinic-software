## Goal
### Why this plan
During shadow use of the installed 0.3.0 a generated note held a quoted line with a number that nobody said. The practitioner's own check of the app's screens and a read-only code investigation (recorded off the repository, in the side-session notes) traced it: the line is in the transcript as a short VAD segment, its words uncertainty-marked, directly after a segment that produced no words; Whisper invented it over background sound, shaped by the primer's example sentence ("Pain seven out of ten.", `transcription.py:190-201`); the shipped cue "out of ten" routed it to a section any speaker may fill; its only review flag was one `low_confidence_source` among many, grouped per code and cleared by one click of Acknowledge all. Music in the room — singing especially — passes the voice detector as speech and is the likeliest trigger. No hallucination or prompt-echo guard exists anywhere in `src/`.

### What the practitioner decided (2026-10-09, Plan Mode and `/review-plan`)
- **0.1 Guard policy — flag and route.** A suspect segment's words stay in the transcript, marked; a cue may still route the line into the note, where it carries a visible per-line mark and its own review warning that Acknowledge all cannot clear. Nothing is ever dropped silently.
- **0.2 Number mark — every quoted line with a number** (the transcript's own `is_number_token` rule), one new review warning acknowledged once per note.
- **0.3 Sequencing — after note-routing 0.3.1, as 0.3.2**, before ui-refresh's 0.4.0. Interim operating rule from today: background music off or as low as practical while recording; shadow mode on.
- **0.5 Copy renders WITHOUT the check marks** (the pre-filled mark travels with Copy as today; the Cliniko Write stays bare). Copy is possible only after every review group is acknowledged, so the marks have done their job by then.
- At `/review-plan` (four lenses, every finding code-verified): faster-whisper's `hallucination_silence_threshold` EXCLUDED (a silent drop inside the library at exactly the app's window edges — decision 0.1's rejected outcome); the harness's "uncertainty surfaced" metric left untouched (widening it could only mask a regression); a second voice detector or a music-detector model excluded; human-voice calibration of the rate bound excluded (C1); the pydantic floor raised to `>=2.11` for `Field(exclude_if=…)`; the plan is named `invented-text-guard`.

### What done looks like
A build of record 0.3.2 under which: (1) the primer carries no example sentence, no number and no routing cue, pinned by test; (2) every VAD segment, on the live and the batch path alike, is judged by ONE guard — words per second, mean word probability, a primer echo, the decode-window `no_speech_prob` and the previous segment's emptiness, under preconditions that keep a short real utterance safe — against constants measured beforehand on synthetic audio only; a suspect segment's words are kept and marked `suspect` in the transcript (never dropped) and shown prefixed wherever a segment is quoted; (3) a quoted note line holding a suspect word, or a number, carries a visible per-line mark in the Clean body, the verbatim label and the line editor's row (a section-level mark under a prose style) under two new review codes that Acknowledge all never clears and Copy does not carry; no other reader of the transcript ACTS on a suspect word (Check 4, the autofill and prefill triggers, phrase learning, the spoken pause), and the readers that deliberately still do are named; (4) the clinician review guide carries the music rule, the marks and the Save gate; the threat model, data-flow map, retention schedule and pilot documents describe the guard and its residues; the findings register carries the finding; (5) run 4 of the validation harness (rule v1 as amended 2026-10-09, untouched) at this plan's hardening commit shows a pooled word error rate no worse than run 3's and ZERO suspect segments over the 50 synthetic encounters; the practitioner's music-only developer-build recording yields only suspect-marked segments (counts reported, never text); the replay tool's new suspect-segment count over the kept recordings is recorded.
